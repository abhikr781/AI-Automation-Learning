# Day 16 – AI Lead Qualification & CRM Automation (V2 Plan)

## 🎯 Goal

Improve the V1 workflow and make it closer to a real production system.

---

## Planned Improvements

### 1. Multiple Lead Sources

Support:

- Website Form
- Gmail
- Webhook
- WhatsApp

instead of only one input source.

---

### 2. Better Information Extraction

Separate:

- Company Name
- Industry
- Project Timeline
- Support Duration

to improve AI accuracy.

---

### 3. AI Lead Summary

Generate a short summary like:

"AI Automation project for a finance company with an urgent 2-week timeline."

instead of sending the full requirement.

---

### 4. CRM Integration

Instead of only Google Sheets:

- Airtable
- HubSpot
- Notion
- Zoho CRM

---

### 5. Email Notification

Automatically send an email to the sales team for Hot leads.

---

### 6. Confidence Score

Ask AI to return:

- Confidence Score

This helps when the extracted information is incomplete.

---

### 7. Better Priority Logic

Separate:

- Lead Quality
- Business Priority

This allows more flexible business rules.

---

### 8. Activity Logging

Store every lead with:

- Date
- Time
- Source
- Status

for reporting.

---

### 9. Dashboard

Create a dashboard showing:

- Total Leads
- Hot Leads
- Warm Leads
- Cold Leads
- Conversion Rate

---

### 10. Error Handling

Handle cases like:

- Missing Budget
- Missing Timeline
- Invalid Input
- AI Response Failure
