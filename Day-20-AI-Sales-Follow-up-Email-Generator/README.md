# Day 20 – AI Follow-up Email Agent

## 📌 Project Overview

This workflow automatically generates personalized follow-up emails based on client conversations and sales stage.

AI understands the client's intent and prepares a professional business email.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Writing follow-up emails manually takes time and leads to inconsistent communication.

AI can generate context-aware emails within seconds.

This workflow helps by:

- Understanding client messages
- Detecting client intent
- Writing personalized follow-up emails
- Updating CRM records
- Maintaining follow-up history

---
